## Why the result is not a causal verdict

- The control group is drawn from `trending/verified` while the signal comes from feed; the two universes are not
  matched, and the network distribution is unbalanced.
- A control that receives a signal before its window is labeled may be promoted in `watchlist` and its control
  row is lost; this is selective deletion that cannot be repaired retroactively from the current table.
- The `no_entry` difference means the `status='ok'` analysis compares two parts that differ in
  priceability. Fisher diagnoses the problem and does not cure its bias.
- `token_class` classification derives from all available observations and may change later. That is why this
  report is an auditable snapshot, not a pre-registered immutable sample.
- Mann–Whitney measures distribution ordering, not the median difference specifically, and rank-biserial is a
  paired-ordering effect. The median criterion is separate and computed with a bootstrap interval.
