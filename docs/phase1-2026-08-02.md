## The fixed criterion

- Smallest median difference worth pursuing: **5 percentage points** in favor of the signal.
- Passing the gate requires a meaningful edge in **both median and win rate**.
- One-sided Mann–Whitney U (`signal` ranks higher than `control`) at `alpha=0.05`.
- Power sensitivity check for the Mann–Whitney component only. It assumes, explicitly and narrowly,
  a +5-point location shift for the entire control distribution; this is **not the power of the composite
  gate**, and it does not establish power for every possible effect shape.
- 95% bootstrap confidence intervals. No t-test because of the severe skew.

- **Phase 1 gate status: not passed.** The data shows no edge in both median and
  win rate, and the network mismatch and missing entries prevent interpreting the difference as the signal's

## Why the result is not a causal verdict

- The control group is drawn from `trending/verified` while the signal comes from feed; the two universes are not
  matched, and the network distribution is unbalanced.
- A control that receives a signal before its window is labeled may be promoted in `watchlist` and its control
  row is lost; this is selective deletion that cannot be repaired retroactively from the current table.
- The `no_entry` difference means the `status='ok'` analysis compares two parts that differ in
  priceability. Fisher diagnoses the problem and does not cure its bias.
- `token_class` classification derives from all available observations and may change later. That is why this
  report is an auditable snapshot, not a pre-registered immutable sample.
- Mann–Whitney measures distribution ordering, not the median difference specifically, and rank-biserial is a
  paired-ordering effect. The median criterion is separate and computed with a bootstrap interval.

## Later corrective action for this report

The `watch_windows` v2 collection design was applied on 2026-08-02 after this measurement. Then the
old control group was permanently deleted from the operating database and its local backups by the owner's
decision; this report's historical numbers do not change, but they can no longer be recomputed from the
operating database. v2 keeps every window without replacement, records the acceptance price for both sides,
matches networks cumulatively, and prevents labeling before the final pull. The re-test will run only on
`design_version>=3`. A preliminary safety check runs at ≥100 controls and ≥5 days, but the gate
decision is not re-made before ≥500 mature controls and ≥14 days, with a sensitivity analysis for the biggest
winner and the top 1% and a stability check across time and networks.
